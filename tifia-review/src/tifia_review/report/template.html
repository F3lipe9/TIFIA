Method note

Text. Both PDFs are read page by page with PyMuPDF. Running headers and footers, page numbers and the template's drafting footnotes are removed, and quote marks, dashes and ligatures are made uniform, so formatting differences do not show up as changes.

Sections. Each document is split at its headings (Sections, lettered and numbered clauses, schedules and exhibits). Template sections are paired with agreement sections by number, then by caption, then by meaning (sentence embeddings, threshold {{ r.meta.threshold }}). Of {{ r.alignment.values()|sum }} pairings: {% for k, v in r.alignment.items() %}{{ v }} {{ k }}{{ ", " if not loop.last else "." }}{% endfor %} A clause that was merged into another or split in two shows up as one section removed and one added.

Deviations. Paired sections are compared sentence by sentence, and definitions term by term. A sentence that differs only where the template has a blank or bracketed option is a fill-in, not a negotiated change. A section that moved to another number is listed as renumbered.

Classification. {{ r.meta.classification_method }}

Citations. Pages are the page numbers printed on the documents, with the PDF page in brackets when it differs.

Limits. This is a Phase 0 prototype. Pages without a text layer are not compared. Tables are compared as text in reading order. The categories and the definition of a substantive change are drafts until the advisor confirms them.
